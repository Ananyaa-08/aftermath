# Homepage visual research

Research performed 1–2 November 2026. The result combines techniques rather than reproducing a particular site. No reference imagery, logos, or signature illustrations were copied.

| Reference | Useful technique | Boundary and AFTERMATH adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Fraunces by Undercase Type](https://fraunces.undercase.xyz/) | A soft old-style variable serif with optical-size, softness, weight, and wonky axes gives oversized words crafted character. | The official font is self-hosted and used for the lowercase wordmark and editorial headings. No commercial typeface is imitated. Fraunces is SIL Open Font License 1.1; local license text is retained. |
| [MIT Media Lab identity by Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/mit-media-lab) | A consistent underlying grid can make scientific and interdisciplinary material feel systematic while supporting variation. | AFTERMATH uses rules, registers, figure captions, and aligned sections. It does not reuse the Media Lab grid glyph, logo, imagery, or group system. |
| [It’s Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/) | Dense editorial taxonomy, magazine pacing, strong type hierarchy, and visual culture presented as an archive. | The homepage uses compact editorial navigation, section indices, asymmetrical pacing, and a capability register. It does not copy articles, images, branding, or page compositions. |
| User-provided “salty” reference | Cropped display type, image/type overlap, narrow navigation, a main visual field paired with a descriptive side column. | The giant lowercase wordmark leads into a two-column hero with an original code-built source-revision collage and an honest availability note. |
| User-provided “essays” reference | Soft, irregular, heavy serif character and tight wordmark spacing. | Fraunces uses its licensed softness and wonky axes for a readable `aftermath` wordmark. Letterforms were not traced or recreated from the reference. |

The focal image is original HTML/CSS: paper fragments, revision lines, an archived-answer slip, grid, grain, and annotations. It is labeled conceptual and does not present evidence or live activity. The collage remains within sky `#B8E7F2`, paper `#FFF9E8`, and ganache `#382414`. Tonal variations use opacity or `color-mix` from those pigments.

Responsive research focused on preserving the complete word at small sizes, allowing the collage to lead before the explanation, stacking later grids, and avoiding horizontal overflow. Motion is limited to a brief wordmark entrance and is removed by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
